## 💡 Usage Instructions

### **For Companies:**
```
1. Register as Company
2. Add branches (optional)
3. Request a ride
4. See available drivers on map
5. Press "Request Ride"
6. Track driver in real-time
7. Chat with driver
8. Rate driver after completion
```

### **For Drivers:**

```
1. Register as Driver
2. Toggle "Активен" ON
3. Wait for ride requests
4. Receive notification popup
5. Accept or Decline
6. Navigate to pickup
7. Complete ride
8. View earnings
```
